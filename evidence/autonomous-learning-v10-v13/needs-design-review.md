# Independent Astra/high review: hunter/gatherer clothing and housing needs

Read-only source review. No native steps, model construction/forward/sampling, optimization, or source edits were executed. The user-authorized native needs change supersedes earlier no-physics-change guidance for this specific mechanic. V12 collection/training remains paused; its staged pins do not authorize running against changed physics.

## Finding: the proposed mechanism is coherent, but the population-loss hypothesis needs precise language

`QoLMortality.cs` uses the previously committed camp QoL to set a nonnegative net annual growth ceiling: max(0,b0−(b0−g)*Q^−a). It explicitly does not subtract or record QoL deaths. At current default b0=.045,g=.01145,a=1, growth stops below Q≈.745556. A low QoL can therefore suppress recovery after population loss, but it is not itself the direct recorded death mechanism.

`GenerationalEconomy.SustainGeneration` computes food deficits and queues actual starvation losses after the configured grace period. Those losses are applied only after production, needs and storage; births planned from the earlier committed QoL are also added after needs. Fission and immigration happen later at Close. Distinguish starvation deaths, no net growth, emigrants and daughter outflows in every counterfactual report. An exemption that raises current resolved QoL affects the next decision's growth ceiling; it must not retroactively change the current turn's planned births or erase starvation.

Relevant source: `Assets/_Anthropocene/Simulation/QoLMortality.cs`, `GenerationalEconomy.cs:353`, `GenerationalEconomy.cs:910`, `TurnResolver.cs:144`.

## Authoritative eligibility and cohort

There is no resident Hunter workforce role. Resident roles are the thirteen entries in `WorkforceRoles.All`. The resident Gatherer cohort is the implicit unassigned workforce plus explicit `WorkforceRoles.Gatherer` assignments: the same accounting already used by agricultural/gathering labor (`c.GathererPopulation + CultivationEconomy.Workers(c, WorkforceRoles.Gatherer)`). Count people in the accepted/prepared allocation, not requested policy proportions, technological era/capability labels, realized harvest output, or the final post-demography population. The policy compiler explicitly records Gatherer assignments, so implicit-only counting would miss neural-assigned gatherers.

For a prepared resident cohort N, define eligible G consistently from that preview/locked workforce and clamp it to[0,N]. The relevant resident count is after accepted dispatch and before this interval's deaths/newborns. General Gatherer material-gathering activity is still within the Gatherer job unless the user explicitly requests a narrower food-only exemption; do not infer exemption from realized food output, which would punish an unsuccessful gatherer despite their job. Farmer, Herder, Fisher, specialists and dedicated extractors are not automatically eligible merely because their camp has Paleolithic ancestry or also contains gatherers.

Dispatched `Hunter` entities are already excluded from camp resident clothing/housing demand. `TurnPlanning.ComputeNeeds` explicitly distinguishes Residents from FoodConsumers: dispatch removes residents but creates no new person; linked hunters and new departures remain biological food consumers. Disconnected hunters consume local/carried provisions and can starve independently. Hunter reintegration occurs after current needs, so it affects the next resident needs cohort. Hunters can hunt, fish, forage or reintegrate through orders; none has its own clothing/housing inventory or QoL field. Preserve this existing exemption rather than add hunters to camp goods denominators or boost the camp's resident QoL by counting them again. Document that their provisioning/starvation and shared growth convention remain unchanged.

## Recommended native rule

Implement an explicit, versioned job-cohort exemption in authoritative needs accounting. Let U=N−G be people still requiring manufactured clothing/shelter. Required fabric consumption is U*FabricConsumptionPerPersonTurn*years/ReferenceTurnYears. Consume actual Cloth then AnimalFabric against that requirement exactly once. Total clothing need coverage is (G+physically_clothed_people)/N, bounded to[0,1], with an explicit empty-cohort convention. Housing need coverage analogously credits G plus real shelter allocated to at most U other people. Thus no-stock coverage is G/N, and an all-Gatherer cohort has both needs met without creating goods.

Do not create Cloth, AnimalFabric, LightHousing, FixedHousing, HousingMaterial, exports or production receipts to represent automatic coverage. Do not modify actual housing capacity or ownership, storage capacity, decay, shelter transferred to daughters, food/water need, equipment wear, crowding policy, or demographic rates. In particular, real `HousingShare` still determines settlement social-cohesion capacity. Automatic housing NEED satisfaction is not constructed infrastructure. At food/water1,100 all-gatherers and zero real shelter, clothing/housing need coverage becomes1 while QoL remains approximately exp(-(100/220)^2)=.8133 under current cohesion defaults. Large unhoused groups can remain growth-limited: the narrow requested mechanic does not guarantee arbitrary settlement size or recovery from ecological food failure.

Preserve the distinction between actual fabric consumed/physical shelter and effective need coverage in receipts and diagnostics. If existing `ClothPerCapita`/`HousingPerCapita` become effective total need coverage, audit every downstream reader and version their meaning; they cannot then be treated as transferable physical goods. Keep enough authoritative cohort/physical-versus-automatic coverage information to evaluate later boundary changes without inventing cloth.

## Integration pitfalls that a one-line QoL patch would miss

* `NeolithicProduction.ResolveNeeds` is only one of the consumers. The same cohort rule must govern `OwnDemand`, clothing useful reserves, `ClothingNeedUnits/ClothingNeedFeedstock`, housing gaps used for need production, `TurnPlanning.ComputeNeeds`, `ProductionDemand`, planner candidate previews and food/feedstock envelopes. Otherwise the planner still diverts food and labor to exempt needs, or forecasts disagree with realization. This may legitimately change production choices; it must not create free inputs or refund already-consumed material.
* Accepted workforce is candidate-dependent. Derive eligibility from the preview camp for forecasts and the same prepared allocation for resolution. Do not subtract dispatch twice or count newborns as this interval's workers. Do not mutate live workforce while evaluating candidates.
* Needs resolve after production and, when applicable, after after-production trade. Goods held/exported and imported physical stocks keep their current chronology. Both resolver paths must use the same exemption exactly once.
* `Fission.QoLModel.Shape` currently treats `camp.ClothPerCapita*Residents` as a dilutable physical amount and recomputes housing from real shelter alone. `EstimateSplit` likewise combines committed clothing with physical housing. These formulas need explicit physical/automatic coverage treatment if coverage is redefined. Otherwise predicted QoL disagrees with realized QoL or grants virtual cloth to noneligible arrivals.
* Immigration removes source workforce proportionally, while destination arrivals become implicit Gatherers. Fission splits workforce proportionally but deliberately does not carry Farmer assignments to daughters, making those people implicit Gatherers. Eligibility must follow projected/committed jobs, not a copied parent coverage ratio or a simple fixed exemption fraction. Do not modify those workforce-transfer rules as part of this change.
* Housing's need coverage and its real-infrastructure social-cohesion contribution must remain distinct across ResolveNeeds, fission, immigration and production-value calculations. Setting `HousingShare=1` for all gatherers would be an additional broad social-physics change that the narrow request does not imply.
* Current QoL may still be low from food, water or crowding. Report component contributions and population-flow decomposition; do not conclude the exemption failed merely because total QoL is below1 or population still falls.

## Versioning recommendation

Use an explicit native configuration mode/flag with legacy behavior when absent/disabled, and enable it explicitly for the new V13 game/experiment configuration. This provides a concrete user-requested mechanic and a paired baseline without silently reinterpreting old V11 checkpoints, rewards, bank snapshots or replay results. New-game default enablement on the experimental branch is reasonable after the focused proof, but old saves must not silently acquire the new rule. No additional user confirmation is necessary for the already-authorized implementation.

Do not assume `[OptionalField]` plus a false default preserves byte-identical legacy fingerprints: this project uses `DataContractSerializer` over serializable fields, which can still emit the new field when false. If exact old config/fingerprint serialization remains a requirement, preserve old serialization branches explicitly—for example a nonserialized runtime flag plus versioned save extension and enabled-mode hash discriminator, following the existing history-extension pattern. Otherwise disclose the fingerprint-format change and retain the old baseline binary; do not claim exact old fingerprints without evidence. Enabled mode must persist through save/load, be included in authoritative configuration/fingerprint/replay identity, and reject unsupported loads rather than silently downgrade.

Action authority and actor input width need not expand for this rule. Native environment/physics and checkpoint provenance must distinguish it; old model8 parameters may be explicitly imported as a candidate, but their old critic calibration/economic outcomes do not establish readiness under changed needs. Preserve all V11 results and do not run the staged V12 plan with new semantics under old pins.

## Required focused tests and bounded counterfactual proof

1. Mixed cohort algebra: e.g.N=100,G=40, no stocks gives both need coverages.4; fabric and shelter for30 additional people give.7, with exactly the nonexempt physical consumption. All-Gatherer gives coverage1 with no goods created or consumed for exemption; G=0 reproduces legacy formulas.
2. Implicit versus explicit Gatherer assignments yield identical eligibility; specialists/Farmer/Herder/Fisher/dedicated extractors are excluded; current accepted reassignment changes eligibility. Include general Gatherer material allocation so job and realized-output definitions cannot drift.
3. Dispatch reduces resident/goods cohort but keeps the correct food cohort. Connected/disconnected hunters retain their existing food starvation behavior and goods exclusion. Reintegration joins next needs cohort. Include accepted economy plus dispatch where legal.
4. Pending starvation deaths and newborns do not alter this turn's locked needs/workforce; direct food-starvation losses still occur. Demonstrate the one-turn lag between improved needs QoL and the growth ceiling using enough food and separately food-limited cases.
5. Planner/forecast versus realized clothing/feedstock/housing demand agree for the same accepted allocation, with and without after-production trade. Inventories, real housing capacity, decay, tools, export escrow and commodity conservation stay physically accounted.
6. Fission/immigration estimators agree with their declared projected job coverage; automatic coverage is not transferred as cloth or houses. Test daughter Farmer-to-Gatherer conversion and immigrant default-Gatherer assignment. Real housing cohesion remains unchanged when only the exemption toggles.
7. Enabled/disabled save-load and replay, worker-count/order determinism, malformed mode handling, history/reward identity and exact legacy regressions appropriate to the chosen serialization contract. No disabled-mode population/QoL difference is allowed.
8. Run a prospectively capped paired native proof from identical snapshots with identical scripted accepted jobs/actions, changing only the needs mode. Separate a food-sufficient isolated cohort from food-scarce and mixed-workforce cases. Capture clothing/housing physical stock and automatic coverage, Food/Water/Cohesion, prior-versus-new QoL, net growth ceiling, starvation, births, emigration and fission at each boundary. This establishes causality without a new PPO campaign. Declare any subsequent neural retraining/calibration budget separately after native proof.
