# Reviewed implementation contract: feasible-request learner v1

Root architectural decision, 23 October 2026. Authorized implementation of ANTHROPOCENE_TRAINING_FINDINGS.md, including requested-versus-executed actor feedback. New opt-in architecture; frozen T3 semantics remain unchanged. This document is not a readiness finding.

## Stage 1: bounded inputs and anchored critics

Preserve legacy normalizer/checkpoint evaluation exactly. New runs persist a feature-contract identifier; mismatched resume fails. Counts/durations use bounded transforms, finite guards and explicit unsupported-history masks. A constant clone-time feature cannot become a huge closed-loop input. Unsupported history may remain masked during support collection/critic warmup; unmasking requires explicit closed-loop evidence and recalibration, not silent mutation of a historical normalizer.

Anchor all enabled value heads to independently observed returns alongside TD targets. A separate calibration set controls actor updates; five warmup iterations alone are not acceptance. Truncation is not termination, an unobserved tail is not zero, and target provenance must bind horizon/discount semantics. Check magnitude and supported within-state action ranking; all-zero targets use tie-aware checks. Missing evidence keeps actor updates closed without preventing critic/support collection. The data producer and training path must be runnable, not disconnected helpers.

New-mode PPO uses one permutation per epoch, includes the short final batch and reports coverage. A fixed rollout audit checks post-step sampled KL; rejected steps restore parameters and optimizer state together. Legacy replay stays unchanged. No local calibration result declares readiness or selects a treatment.

## Stage 2: actor labor authority and bounded retry

Authoritative state is the committed native world and prior cohort at the WEGO boundary. User revision, 23 October: the labor head is a masked specialty softmax over the full controllable settlement population, with an explicit idle category. Previous assignments and native planned reservations do not reduce this pool. Deterministic largest-remainder apportionment preserves total integer headcount; expose the rounding delta without penalizing ordinary rounding. Each multi-output specialty has actor-controlled masked activity shares, including idle. Subsistence/material gathering and operation/development splits must also remain explicit wherever the physical role has those alternatives. This supersedes the earlier 21-task-bundle output proposal.

Actor priorities replace overlapping native discretionary allocation in the new mode. Native execution enforces physical laws, material conservation and recipe dependencies but cannot add upstream workers, transfer idle capacity between roles or goods, or substitute its preferred production mix. Preserve trade/movement chronology, production, demography and hidden-information boundaries. Cost scales with the fixed specialty/activity inventory and active local sources, never population-sized allocation loops.

Specialties and specialty-activity pairs are tokens with stable registry identities, capability/recipe features, availability masks and matching feedback. Shared scoring heads produce masked softmax shares over the supplied tokens. Fourteen specialty coordinates and thirteen by twenty-two activity coordinates describe today's catalog, not bespoke model output layers or a permanent size ceiling. Each specialty aggregates its own activity tokens; each activity remains conditioned on its owning specialty. Activity attention is local to that specialty, with global context exchanged through specialty, resource and village tokens. Padding and batching cannot change meaning or produce nonfinite values; new registries require explicit version compatibility/migration, never silent ordinal reuse. Test shared scoring with an additional synthetic specialty, reordered identity-feature pairs, and absent padded specialty/activity rows. Adding new feature semantics or resource types may require an explicit encoder migration, even when adding a specialty using existing semantics does not require new output parameters. Tokenization alone does not claim a previously untrained specialty is behaviorally ready.

Staffing a material-constrained specialty is legal and can produce unused capacity. It is not an infeasible promise of goods the actor never requested. Let physical execution expose that economic mistake through actual work, output and known bottlenecks; do not reject it merely for low productivity. A production forecast or unconstrained capacity is explicitly a model-derived estimate, not a sampled output target. Hard-invalid requests still use the bounded retry protocol below.

Two proposals maximum: initial plus one labor-only retry with other sampled choices fixed. Validation is read-only, draws no simulation RNG and cannot advance time. Feedback contains constraints, requested/available amounts, affected activities and remaining retries, never native preferred allocations. All villages use the same committed boundary. Accepted allocation executes once; final failure invokes a terminating deterministic minimal fallback preserving actor priorities and explicit idle capacity.

Avoidable rejection costs a positive base plus bounded resource-normalized severity. A separate rescue surcharge cannot repeat severity. Sum costs across attempts. Exclude tolerated rounding, unobserved competition and discretionary overrides; unknown avoidability is not presumed fault. Dedicated feasibility-cost critic stays separate from small-settlement cost and contributes negatively to actor advantage at a declared stable scale.

## Outcome feedback

Actor and task/cost critics receive previous requested, accepted and executed allocation plus signed stage deltas, in common units/activity order, with masks, reasons, avoidability, fallback and parent identity. Preserve requested fractional headcounts, accepted integer staffing and activity worker-year budgets, and actual consumed work separately. Keep gross resource production, material consumption, trade transfers and net inventory change distinct; net stock change is not production. Include resource-specific known bottlenecks and forecast-versus-realized output where a real forecast exists. Never subtract goods units from worker units or present unknown work as zero. Retry input contains rejection feedback only; final execution enters successor physical observation. Keep each attempt's raw request and identify the final sampled proposal as execution's direct comparator.

Real outcomes train the policy-plus-execution system. PPO scores sampled requests, never relabels compiler output as an actor action. Logistic-normal labor sampling uses the stable explicit idle token as its additive-log-ratio reference, not whichever row happens to be last; bind that sampling contract in checkpoints and test non-idle permutation equivalence. Deltas diagnose mediation and support observed avoidable costs; they cannot estimate the counterfactual outcome of an infeasible request.

## Attempt-aware training

Keep every attempt: parent ID, state/masks, sample/latents, old log probability, policy version, task/cost values, validator result, immediate cost and successor. Internal rejection edge: zero task reward, discount 1, GAE continuation 1. Physical edge: exactly one reward and duration with physical discount/trace factor. Final rejection may carry rejection/rescue cost plus eventual resolved outcome. Fallback is an execution record, not actor sample. Retry likelihood includes only resampled labor heads.

Sum attempt losses per physical parent decision. Do not dilute rejection costs by attempt averaging, duplicate rewards, or multiply unrelated teacher/entropy/mix/commitment terms. Final new-mode objectives are personality-conditioned QoL/population with fixed reference scales, explicit constraints and trade diagnostics; historical modes retain their saved welfare/trade semantics.
