## Population, QoL, welfare and trade behavior

Interval welfare is opening focal resident population × realized end-interval
QoL × elapsed years. Annual welfare is its integrated sum divided by measured
years; it is not the product of separately averaged population and QoL. Daughters
run separate native Elders, and their later population/QoL/reward is excluded.
Person-years use opening residents × interval years, not continuous population.

Owner-only closed-turn sidecars report native resident growth/starvation,
migration in/out, daughter headcount and all four fission triggers. Requested and
compiled Gatherer/Farmer/Craftsperson shares, expansion share, food reserve years
and field intensity are reported. OtherResidentChange is an algebraic residual,
not an independent population measurement; unexplained residuals are rejected
in this no-dispatch cohort. Linked hunters are excluded from resident deaths.
Diagnostics do not enter actor/value inputs, reward, state or replay.

## Trade representation, limits and performance

The MLP input includes own log1p-compressed stocks, legal goods/partner masks,
ordered remembered contacts (distance/confidence/trust/permitted trade history),
and the previous requested quote plus realized settlement count and legs in the
native receipt. It receives no foreign inventory, private utility or current
other-policy quote. Six conditional categorical heads select enable, known partner,
give good, different wanted good, lot and exchange ratio. Lots are5/20/50% of
own stock, capped for the native1e−6..1e6 quantity bounds; ratios are.5/1/2.
Escrow limits actual available stock. Microscopic stocks can yield requested
amounts above held stock after lower-bound rounding; authoritative stock-limited
matching, not a policy rescue, determines fill. Exact PPO likelihood sums the
actual masked conditional decisions and exploration mixture, rather than scoring
settled quantities. Quotes can fail legally. The coarse grid, one slot and absence
of current public offer inputs remain limitations.
